# TilesCRM — Tiles, Granite & Marble Operations

TilesCRM is a CRM, inventory, billing and fabrication platform for tile dealers,
granite and marble yards, quartz suppliers, showrooms and installation teams.

## Business lifecycle

Lead or walk-in → application and area capture → lot/slab or tile selection →
quotation → purchase and receiving → godown stock → POS/GST invoice → delivery,
fabrication and installation follow-up.

## Product and inventory

- Tile catalog fields: size, finish, surface, application, coverage per box,
  tiles per box, UOM, HSN and GST rate.
- Stone catalog fields: material type, origin, thickness, grade, book-match
  support and slab tracking.
- Godowns, branches, transfers, stock ledger, opening balances, reorder alerts
  and product stock synchronisation.
- Purchase batches with FIFO-friendly remaining quantities and shade/dye-lot
  codes for tile sales.
- Natural-stone lot explorer with lot number, supplier, origin, shade, QC,
  photos, cost per sq.ft, godown location and individually measured slabs.
- Slab statuses: available, reserved, in processing, sold and damaged.
- Book-match pairing, sample loans, sample return/lost control and QC notes.
- Reusable granite/marble offcuts can be recorded with dimensions, area, shade,
  source slab, source order, photo and resale value.

## Quotations and sales

- Quotations support PCS, BOX, SQFT, SQM, SLAB and RFT pricing.
- Box quotes convert entered area into required boxes using coverage per box.
- Slab quotes and invoices preserve measured area and the selected physical slab.
- POS supports tile batches, shade selection, exact slab selection, godown,
  discounts, split payments, transport, freight, loading and road permits.
- Invoice creation decrements the selected godown and batch, claims the slab and
  writes an auditable stock ledger entry.
- Cancellation and full refund restore stock, batch quantity and sold slabs.
- Held invoices do not consume stock; finalising a held invoice performs the
  same inventory checks as a normal sale.

## GST and documents

- HSN master for tiles, worked stone, rough stone, adhesives and profiles.
- Intrastate CGST/SGST and interstate IGST with place-of-supply capture.
- GSTR-1 HSN summaries use UQC-aware quantities: BOX, SQF, SQM, MTR and NOS.
- Invoice/quotation printouts show UOM, measured area, HSN, rate and line amount.
- E-way bill fields, payment history, credit notes and export/download sharing.

## Custom fabrication

- Site measurements, application type, template method, edge profile, cutouts,
  wastage, reference photos and assigned staff.
- Multi-slab reservation with transactional availability checks.
- Workflow from measurement scheduled to production, QC and delivered.
- Delivered jobs consume reserved slabs; orders moved to drafts release them.
- Manufacturing work centres, production orders, material consumption, job
  costing, scrap/offcut records and custom-order inventory.

## CRM, communication and automation

- Leads, sources, follow-ups, appointments, walk-ins, reviews and customer
  history are linked to contacts.
- WhatsApp, email, Instagram and website conversation workflows use TGM copy.
- AI calling assistant asks for material/application, area, city, timeline and
  contact details; it avoids inventing stock or price and escalates uncertainty.
- AI visual recommendation supports floor, wall, backsplash, countertop,
  staircase and vanity surfaces while preserving room geometry.

## Management controls

- Dashboard KPIs for revenue, collections, conversion, available stone,
  reserved slabs, fabrication jobs and month-to-date sq.ft sold.
- Role permissions for admin, manager and staff.
- Draft/archive safety, audit-friendly ledgers and production verification.
